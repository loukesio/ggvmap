I’m sharing ggvmap, an R package for creating Voronoi treemaps with ggplot2.

Each cell’s area represents its share of a total. You can choose the outline, use built-in palettes, adjust labels, and add group totals with an outer ring—all through a familiar plotting workflow.

The new vmap_region() function also fits values inside real outlines, including bends, holes, and separate islands, using optional geometry packages.

This short demo walks through the options using the reading palette. The budget values are invented for teaching; the Berlin example shows the shape support, not real Berlin statistics.

Code, examples, and installation:
https://github.com/loukesio/ggvmap
